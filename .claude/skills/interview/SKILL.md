# Interview

Read existing requirements before questioning the user. Focus on consequential unknowns:
outcomes, constraints, interfaces, tradeoffs and acceptance criteria. Batch related questions
with an available question tool or ask in conversation. Let dependent questions follow the
answers they need. Stop once the requested specification is actionable; do not prolong the
interview with obvious questions or reopen approved decisions.

Write the agreed spec at the requested path, or `docs/specs/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-design.md`.
Follow the [lifecycle contract](../../references/documentation/lifecycle.md). Preserve
unresolved decisions explicitly and honor any requested review gate. An interview request
does not authorize implementation.
